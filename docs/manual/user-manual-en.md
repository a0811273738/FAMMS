# POS Pro User Manual (English)

> For shop owners and cashier staff. This manual walks you step by step from first login to daily operations.
> For installation, see the Install Guide first (docs/sales/03-Install-Guide-English.md).

---

## 1. First-Time Setup

### 1.1 Log in
1. Tap the POS Pro icon on your home screen
2. Top-right corner: switch language — 中文 / English / Bahasa Indonesia
3. Choose the "Boss" account, enter default password `1234`
4. Tap "Log in"

### 1.2 Change the passwords immediately (important!)
1. Open "Settings" in the left menu
2. Find "Staff Accounts"
3. Change the passwords for both "Boss" and "Staff"
4. Share the new passwords only with people who need them

### 1.3 Set your shop name
1. "Settings" → "Store Info"
2. Enter your shop name (it will be printed on receipts)

---

## 2. Adding Products

### 2.1 Add one product manually
1. Open "Inventory" in the left menu
2. Tap "Add Product"
3. Fill in: product name, selling price, cost (optional), stock quantity
4. For barcoded items: tap the "Barcode" field and scan with your scanner, or type the number
5. Tap "Save"

### 2.2 Import many products at once (CSV)
1. "Inventory" → "Export CSV" to download the template format
2. Open it in Excel and fill in your products
3. "Inventory" → "Import CSV" and choose your file
4. Check the preview, then confirm the import

💡 Tip: enter your 30–50 best-selling items first and start selling — add the rest over time.

---

## 3. Checkout (Your Daily Routine)

### 3.1 Open a shift
1. Before the first sale, go to "Shifts" and tap "Open Shift"
2. Enter the cash float in your drawer (e.g., 500)
3. Now you can start selling

### 3.2 Ring up a sale
1. Open "POS" in the left menu
2. Add items to the cart — three ways:
   - **Scan barcode**: use a scanner gun, or tap "Camera Scan" to use the phone camera
   - **Search**: type the product name in the search bar
   - **Browse**: tap a category tab, then tap the product
3. Adjust quantity with "+" / "−" in the cart
4. Tap "Checkout"
5. Choose payment method (cash etc.), enter the amount received
6. The screen shows the **change to give** — confirm to finish
7. Tap "Print" if the customer wants a receipt

### 3.3 Hold an order (customer steps away)
1. With items in the cart, tap "Hold"
2. Serve the next customer
3. When they return, open the "Held Orders" list and continue their sale

### 3.4 Refunds
1. Find the transaction in "Orders"
2. Tap "Refund" and tick the items being returned
3. Confirm the amount — stock and points are restored automatically

### 3.5 Close the shift (end of day)
1. "Shifts" → "Close Shift"
2. Count the cash in the drawer and enter the actual amount
3. The system calculates any difference and shows the shift report

---

## 4. Inventory Management

- **Low-stock alerts**: items below their safety stock show a warning — time to restock
- **Purchasing**: "Purchasing" → choose supplier → create purchase order → tap "Receive" on arrival; stock updates automatically
- **Stocktake**: "Stocktake" → count and enter actual quantities → the system lists and fixes differences
- **Expiry warnings**: items with expiry dates warn you before they expire

---

## 5. Members (Regular Customers)

### 5.1 Create a member
1. "Members" → "Add Member"
2. Enter name and phone number (the phone number doubles as the member ID)

### 5.2 Earn points at checkout
1. Before checkout, tap the "Member" field and look up by phone number
2. Points accumulate automatically and can be redeemed next time

### 5.3 Store credit (deposit)
1. "Members" → select a member → "Deposit"
2. Take the cash, enter the amount — future purchases can be paid from the balance

---

## 6. Scan-to-Order (For Eateries)

1. "Settings" → enable "QR Ordering"
2. Generate the QR code, print it, and stick one on each table (with table number)
3. Customers scan with their phone → pick dishes → submit
4. Orders appear in your "Orders" page — cook, serve, and collect payment

---

## 7. Reports (For the Owner)

- **Dashboard**: today's revenue, gross profit, and transaction count right after login
- **Reports**: daily/monthly revenue trends, best sellers, slow movers, staff performance
- **Accounting**: automatic bookkeeping with profit & loss statement; "Export CSV" for your accountant

---

## 8. Back Up Your Data (Do This Weekly!)

1. "Settings" → "Export" to save your data as CSV files
2. Send them to your own Google Drive or computer
3. Shops with the cloud add-on: data backs up automatically and syncs across devices

---

## 9. FAQ & Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Forgot password | Contact support for a reset |
| Barcode won't scan | Check the product exists and the barcode is correct; camera scan needs camera permission |
| Can't find a feature | Some advanced features live in "Full Mode" — switch in "Settings" |
| Switching to a new phone | "Export" a backup first → contact support to unbind → install on the new phone and "Import" |
| Screen looks wrong | Fully close and reopen the app; if it persists, refresh |
