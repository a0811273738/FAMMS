# POS Pro Sales Playbook (English)

> For sales reps: everything you need to start selling today — product pitch, pricing, demo flow, objection handling, and installation steps.

---

## 1. What You're Selling (memorize this 30-second pitch)

POS Pro is a **cashier app** for small shops, installed on a phone or tablet:
- **Pay once, own it forever — NO monthly fee** (every competitor charges monthly; this is your biggest weapon)
- **Works without internet** (internet is only needed once, during installation)
- Checkout, inventory, members, and reports — all included
- For eateries: **customers scan a QR code on the table and order by themselves** — saves one waiter

## 2. Who to Sell To

| Customer | Pain | Lead features |
|----------|------|---------------|
| Grocery stalls (warung) | Paper bookkeeping, no idea of profit, frequent stockouts | Barcode checkout, daily report, low-stock alerts |
| Small eateries (rumah makan) | Shouted orders, wrong bills, short-staffed | QR self-ordering, kitchen tickets, QRIS payment |

## 4. The 5-Minute Demo (use your own demo device)

1. **Opening question**: "Boss, how do you track sales right now?" (let them say notebook / Excel / nothing)
2. **Ring up a sale in 30 seconds**: scan barcode → take payment → change screen
3. **Show today's report**: "Revenue and profit, visible any time"
4. **For eateries, add**: put down your QR stand, scan with your own phone → order → the ticket appears
5. **Killer line**: "Pay once. No monthly fee, ever. Works even when the internet is down."
6. **Close on the spot**: take payment → install in 5 minutes → teach the basics

## 5. Objection Handling

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| Too expensive | "Other POS systems charge 【competitor monthly fee】 per month. This one you pay once and use for years — it pays for itself in two months." |
| I'm not good with tech | "I'll install it right now and teach you until you've got it. After that, WhatsApp me any time." |
| What if I lose my data? | "Your data lives on your own phone, and you can export a backup any time." |
| Let me think about it | "I'll send the demo video to your WhatsApp and come back tomorrow around this time." (always capture the contact + book the follow-up) |

## 6. Installing for the Customer (3 steps, 5 minutes)

1. Open 【INSTALL-URL】 in the customer's phone browser → "Add to Home Screen"
2. Open the app, enter the activation code (request from HQ — one code per sale)
3. Choose language → change the default password → walk them through one sale

Always teach these three things before leaving: **checkout, today's revenue, export backup**.

## 7. After-Sales Rules

- For customer issues, check the User Manual first; escalate to HQ support WhatsApp: 【NUMBER】
- Phone replacement / device unbinding: forward to HQ
- **Never** promise custom features or refunds — report to HQ and let them decide

## 8. Daily Reporting

After each sale report: shop name, customer WhatsApp, activation code ID, package. HQ uses this to reconcile and pay your commission.
